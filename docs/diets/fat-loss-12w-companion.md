# Fat Loss 12-Week Concurrent Block — 14-Day Companion Diet

Coach- and chef-built nutrition program engineered for the TJFit Fat Loss 12-Week Concurrent Block (4 lifts + 2 conditioning + 1 long walk + 1 rest). This is a deficit. It is not a cleanse, not a detox, not a magic macro split. Numbers are calibrated to strip 350–450 g of fat per week from an intermediate male lifter at an 80 kg reference body without bleeding strength. Protein is high. Vegetables are high. Calories are honest. Cook the food. Hit the macros. Walk every day. The plan minimizes suffering; it does not eliminate it. Hunger is part of the deal — we manage it with volume, fiber, protein, water, sleep, caffeine, and structure.

---

## 1. Overview & Macros

### 1.1 Estimating maintenance calories

Use Mifflin–St Jeor for resting metabolic rate (RMR), then apply an activity multiplier. Track for two weeks before trusting any equation; the equation is a starting point, not a verdict.

**Mifflin–St Jeor (men):** RMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) + 5
**Mifflin–St Jeor (women):** RMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) − 161

Activity multiplier for this program (4 lifts + 2 conditioning + 1 long walk, desk job otherwise): **1.55**.

**Worked example — 80 kg, 180 cm, 28-year-old male:**
- RMR = (10 × 80) + (6.25 × 180) − (5 × 28) + 5 = 800 + 1125 − 140 + 5 = **1790 kcal**
- Maintenance = 1790 × 1.55 = **2775 kcal**
- Fat-loss deficit = −450 kcal → **Target = 2325 kcal/day**

Deficit prescription: **−400 kcal** if you are already lean (sub-15% BF) or female, **−450 kcal** for the standard male presentation, **−500 kcal** only if BMI ≥ 28 and you have margin to give. Hard cap: **450 g/week (1 lb) scale loss averaged over 4 weeks**. Anything faster shreds muscle, tanks recovery, and breaks the program.

### 1.2 Macro split — training days (6/wk)

35% carbs / 40% protein / 25% fat. Protein anchored at **2.4 g/kg**.

| Tier | BW | kcal | Protein (g) | Carbs (g) | Fat (g) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 65 kg | 1900 | 170 | 165 | 53 |
| Reference | 80 kg | 2325 | 195 | 205 | 65 |
| Heavy | 95 kg | 2750 | 230 | 240 | 76 |

### 1.3 Macro split — rest days (Day 7, Day 14)

Drop carbs ~60 g, hold protein and fat steady. 22% carbs / 47% protein / 31% fat. Sodium up. Walk anyway — 8–10k steps minimum.

| Tier | BW | kcal | Protein (g) | Carbs (g) | Fat (g) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 65 kg | 1700 | 170 | 95 | 60 |
| Reference | 80 kg | 2100 | 195 | 115 | 73 |
| Heavy | 95 kg | 2500 | 230 | 140 | 85 |

### 1.4 Macro split — refeed day (Day 5, Day 12; starts Week 5)

Eat at maintenance. Push carbs to **4.5 g/kg**. Drop fat under 50 g. This is not a cheat day. It is a structured restoration of leptin, thyroid output, glycogen, and training output. Use it on the heaviest training day of the week (Lower or Pull).

| Tier | BW | kcal | Protein (g) | Carbs (g) | Fat (g) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 65 kg | 2250 | 145 | 295 | 42 |
| Reference | 80 kg | 2775 | 175 | 360 | 48 |
| Heavy | 95 kg | 3300 | 205 | 425 | 55 |

Note: refeed protein drops to 2.2 g/kg because carbs do the muscle-sparing work. Fat goes low to make room for the carbs without exceeding maintenance.

### 1.5 Meal timing

- **4 meals/day**: Breakfast (B), Lunch (L), Pre-workout snack (P), Dinner (D). No mandatory post-workout shake in a deficit — your next whole-food meal does the job.
- Breakfast within 60 min of waking. Protein first.
- Pre-workout snack 60–90 min before lifting: 30–45 g carbs + 25–30 g protein, fat under 10 g. Skip the carb portion of P on rest day and on conditioning-only days; eat just the protein + vegetables.
- Last meal 90 min before bed. Bias toward casein-style slow protein (cottage cheese, Greek yogurt) to blunt overnight hunger.
- Space meals 3.5–4 hours apart. Long gaps make compliance worse, not better.
- If you train fasted (early AM), front-load lunch to 600–700 kcal and shrink breakfast — but never under 30 g protein.

### 1.6 Hydration, electrolytes, fiber, micronutrients

- **Water:** 40 ml/kg/day floor (higher than the bulk plan; satiety + thermic dilution). 80 kg → 3.2 L. Add 500–750 ml per hour of training.
- **Sodium:** 4–6 g/day. Salt aggressively. Deficits drop blood pressure and waste sodium; under-salting causes headaches, cramps, and false hunger.
- **Potassium:** 4 g/day. Spinach, potatoes, bananas, yogurt, beans, avocado.
- **Magnesium:** 400 mg/day. Pumpkin seeds, almonds, dark chocolate, supplement if needed.
- **Fiber:** **35–45 g/day floor**, non-negotiable. Fiber is the single best satiety lever you have.
- **Micronutrient floor:** 2 fruits + **600 g vegetables/day** (a third more than the hypertrophy plan). One serving leafy greens, one cruciferous, one colored, daily.

### 1.7 Caffeine and diet sodas as tools

Both are useful, both are honest. Use them.

- **Caffeine:** 3–6 mg/kg/day, last dose ≥ 8 hours before bed. Bumps training output, blunts hunger, improves fat oxidation acutely. Coffee, espresso, green tea, pre-workout — all valid. Cap at 400 mg/day for most adults.
- **Diet soda / zero drinks:** Free. Use them when you want sweet and don't want calories. Aspartame, sucralose, stevia, ace-K are safe at consumed doses. Diet sodas at dinner significantly improve adherence for most people.
- **Tea (green, black, herbal):** Unlimited. Mint tea after dinner is one of the most reliable hunger killers known.
- **Gum:** Sugar-free gum at meal-prep, in the car, late evening. Cheap insurance.

## 2. The 14-Day Meal Plan

All macros target the **80 kg reference tier (2325 kcal training / 2100 kcal rest / 2775 kcal refeed)**. Scale linearly by bodyweight ratio (65 kg → multiply gram weights by 0.81; 95 kg → multiply by 1.19). Day 7 and Day 14 are rest days (lower carbs). Day 5 and Day 12 are refeed days (higher carbs, lower fat).

Meal slot key: **B** = Breakfast, **L** = Lunch, **P** = Pre-workout snack, **D** = Dinner.

### Day 1 (Upper) — Mediterranean

**1B — Smoked Salmon & Cottage Cheese Toast** | 430 kcal · P 42 · C 38 · F 12
- 80 g sourdough (2 slices), 150 g 2% cottage cheese, 60 g cold-smoked salmon, 80 g cucumber, 20 g red onion, 5 g capers, 2 g dill, 10 ml lemon juice, 1 g black pepper, 1 g flaky salt.
- Cook time: 6 min. Easy.
1. Toast sourdough until firm, 3 min.
2. Stir cottage cheese with dill, pepper, half the lemon juice.
3. Spread 75 g cottage cheese per slice.
4. Drape 30 g salmon over each slice.
5. Top with sliced cucumber and onion, scatter capers.
6. Finish with remaining lemon juice and flaky salt.
- Swap 1: cottage cheese → 0% Greek yogurt at 180 g.
- Swap 2: salmon → 70 g chicken breast, sliced thin.
- Coach: cottage cheese has the casein curve. Eat it slow.

**1L — Chicken Shawarma Bowl** | 580 kcal · P 55 · C 55 · F 16
- 180 g chicken breast, 150 g cooked basmati (50 g dry), 200 g romaine, 80 g cucumber, 80 g tomato, 60 g red cabbage (shredded), 30 g pickled turnip, 80 g low-fat tzatziki (recipe §5.5), 5 g shawarma spice (recipe §5.4), 10 ml olive oil, 5 ml lemon, 1 g salt.
- Cook time: 18 min. Easy.
1. Cube chicken, toss with shawarma spice, 5 ml olive oil, salt.
2. Sear in a hot dry pan 4 min per side until charred edges, 73°C / 165°F internal.
3. Cook rice with 1 g salt per 50 g dry, simmer 12 min covered, rest 5.
4. Shred romaine and cabbage, slice cucumber and tomato.
5. Build bowl: rice base, vegetables fanned, chicken on top.
6. Spoon tzatziki across, scatter turnip, finish with remaining oil, lemon, salt.
- Swap 1: chicken → 200 g 95% lean ground turkey.
- Swap 2: rice → 200 g cooked freekeh (60 g dry).
- Coach: shawarma spice is half the meal. Toast it 30 sec in the dry pan before adding chicken.

**1P — Apple, Whey, Almonds** | 320 kcal · P 32 · C 32 · F 8
- 1 medium apple (180 g), 30 g whey isolate in 300 ml water, 12 g raw almonds.
- Cook time: 1 min. Easy.
1. Shake whey in 300 ml cold water 20 sec.
2. Slice apple.
3. Count out almonds (about 10).
- Swap 1: apple → 1 pear (180 g).
- Swap 2: whey → 200 g 0% Greek yogurt + 5 g honey.
- Coach: this lands 75 min before the Upper session. No fat-heavy snacks pre-lift.

**1D — Sheet-Pan Lemon-Herb Cod & Greens** | 480 kcal · P 48 · C 38 · F 14
- 200 g cod loin, 300 g broccoli florets, 200 g cherry tomatoes, 180 g baby potatoes (halved), 10 ml olive oil, 8 g garlic (minced), 1 lemon (zest + 15 ml juice), 3 g oregano, 2 g paprika, 2 g salt, 1 g black pepper, 5 g parsley.
- Cook time: 28 min. Easy.
1. Heat oven to 220°C / 425°F.
2. Toss potatoes with 5 ml oil, salt; roast 12 min on a sheet pan.
3. Pat cod dry, season with paprika, lemon zest, half the garlic, salt, pepper.
4. Add broccoli, tomatoes, remaining garlic, remaining oil to the pan; toss.
5. Lay cod on top; roast 12 min until cod flakes at 60°C / 140°F.
6. Finish with oregano, lemon juice, chopped parsley.
- Swap 1: cod → 200 g haddock or pollock.
- Swap 2: potatoes → 200 g roasted carrots and parsnips.
- Coach: cod overcooks in seconds. Pull at 60°C / 140°F; carryover finishes it.

### Day 4 (Pull) — Indian

## 5. Cooking Primer

### 5.1 Sunday batch-cook playbook (90 min, sets up 4 days)

1. **Roast 1 kg chicken breast** — split, pound to even thickness, brine 30 min in 1 L water + 30 g salt + 20 g sugar, dry, season with paprika + garlic powder + salt, roast at 200°C / 400°F 20 min until 73°C / 165°F. Slice cold, store.
2. **Cook rice base** — 200 g jasmine + 200 g brown, separate pots, 12 min each, fan-cool spread on a tray.
3. **Hard-boil 8 eggs** — boil 9 min, ice bath, peel. Store unpeeled if possible.
4. **Pre-cut vegetables** — 400 g broccoli florets, 300 g bell pepper, 300 g cucumber, 300 g cherry tomatoes, 200 g red onion. Store in glass.
5. **Make 2 master sauces** — low-fat tzatziki (200 g) and chimichurri (150 g). Recipes §5.5.
6. **Toast spice blends** — make 2 weeks' worth of shawarma + taco blends in a dry pan 90 sec, jar.
7. **Portion whey** — pre-bag scoops for the week. Add measured almonds/walnuts to small zip bags.

### 5.2 Storage rules

| Food | Fridge (4°C / 40°F) | Freezer |
|---|---|---|
| Cooked chicken breast | 3 days | 2 months |
| Cooked rice / grains | 4 days | 1 month |
| Hard-boiled eggs (peeled) | 5 days | Do not freeze |
| Cooked fish | 2 days | 1 month |
| Cooked ground meat | 3 days | 2 months |
| Cut vegetables (raw) | 4 days | N/A |
| Greek yogurt / cottage cheese (opened) | 7 days | N/A |
| Fresh herbs | 5 days wrapped in damp paper | Chop, oil-cube, freeze 3 months |
| Hummus (homemade) | 5 days | 1 month |
| Tzatziki | 4 days | Do not freeze |
| Chimichurri | 7 days | 3 months |

Reheat rice with a wet paper towel over the bowl, microwave 90 sec — keeps it from drying out. Reheat lean meat in a pan with a splash of water + lid, low heat, 3 min; never microwave chicken breast unsupervised.

### 5.3 Knife and pan minimums

- 8-inch chef's knife (sharp; sharpen weekly with a ceramic rod).
- Wooden cutting board (large, real one).
- 12-inch cast iron skillet.
- 12-inch non-stick pan.
- Heavy sheet pan.
- Wok (carbon steel, $30, pays for itself in week 2).
- Dutch oven (Day 14 braise).
- Digital meat thermometer. Non-negotiable. Chicken at 73°C / 165°F, fish at 60°C / 140°F, beef rare 50°C / 122°F, medium-rare 54°C / 130°F, medium 60°C / 140°F, ground meats at 71°C / 160°F.

### 5.4 Eight spice blends (exact ratios)

All measured by weight in grams.

1. **Shawarma**: cumin 4, coriander 3, paprika 3, turmeric 1, cinnamon 1, allspice 1, black pepper 1, cardamom 0.5, clove 0.5.
2. **Taco / Tex-Mex**: chili powder 5, cumin 3, paprika 2, garlic powder 2, onion powder 2, oregano 1, salt 1, cayenne 0.5.
3. **Garam masala**: cumin 4, coriander 4, cardamom 2, cinnamon 2, clove 1, black pepper 1, nutmeg 0.5, bay 0.5.
4. **Italian herb blend**: oregano 4, basil 4, thyme 2, rosemary 2, marjoram 1, garlic powder 1.
5. **Cajun**: paprika 5, garlic powder 3, onion powder 3, oregano 2, thyme 2, cayenne 2, black pepper 2, white pepper 1.
6. **Jerk**: allspice 4, thyme 3, garlic powder 2, onion powder 2, brown sugar 2, black pepper 2, cinnamon 1, nutmeg 1, scotch bonnet powder 1, salt 1.
7. **Ras el hanout**: cumin 3, coriander 3, ginger 2, cinnamon 2, paprika 2, turmeric 1, black pepper 1, allspice 1, cardamom 1, clove 0.5, nutmeg 0.5.
8. **Herbes de Provence**: thyme 4, oregano 3, savory 2, rosemary 2, marjoram 1, lavender 0.5 (real; not soap).

Toast all blends in a dry pan 60 sec before storing in airtight jars. Use 5–8 g per protein portion.

### 5.5 Five universal low-cal, high-flavor sauces

1. **Low-fat tzatziki** (per 200 g serve: 110 kcal, 14 P, 7 C, 3 F): 200 g 0% Greek yogurt, 80 g grated cucumber (salted, squeezed), 5 g garlic (microplaned), 10 ml lemon juice, 5 g dill, 1 g salt, 1 g white pepper. Stir. Rest 20 min for flavors.
2. **Salsa verde** (per 200 g: 80 kcal, 2 P, 18 C, 0 F): 300 g tomatillos roasted, 1 jalapeño, 30 g onion, 5 g garlic, 15 g cilantro, 15 ml lime juice, 2 g salt. Blend. Pourable.
3. **Chimichurri** (per 100 g: 200 kcal, 1 P, 4 C, 20 F): 80 g flat-leaf parsley, 20 g oregano, 8 g garlic, 50 ml olive oil, 20 ml red wine vinegar, 2 g chili flakes, 2 g salt. Pulse. Use 20 g per portion.
4. **Mustard vinaigrette** (per 100 ml: 380 kcal, 1 P, 4 C, 38 F): 30 ml olive oil, 30 ml red wine vinegar, 15 g Dijon, 10 g honey, 5 g garlic, 1 g salt, 1 g pepper. Whisk or shake in a jar. Use 15 g per salad.
5. **Soy-ginger** (per 100 ml: 90 kcal, 4 P, 14 C, 2 F): 40 ml soy sauce (low-sodium), 15 ml rice vinegar, 10 g grated ginger, 5 g garlic, 5 g honey, 5 g sesame oil, 5 g sesame seeds, 30 ml water. Whisk.

Make all five Sunday. They live 5–7 days and rescue any plain protein-and-vegetable plate.

## 7. Supplements

### 7.1 Tier 1 — actually worth it

1. **Creatine monohydrate** — 5 g/day, every day, in any drink. Single most evidence-backed legal performance supplement. Maintains strength and lean mass in a deficit. ~$0.10/day. (Kreider et al. 2017, ISSN position stand.)
2. **Whey protein isolate** — 25–35 g/serve. Convenience tool for hitting 2.4 g/kg in a deficit, not a magic ingredient. Isolate over concentrate if lactose sensitive.
3. **Vitamin D3** — 1000–2000 IU/day if sunlight is limited or 25-OH-D blood level is below 30 ng/mL. Get a blood test, then dose.
4. **Omega-3 (EPA+DHA)** — 1–2 g combined EPA+DHA from fish oil, if you don't eat 2 fatty fish meals/week. Look at the EPA+DHA on the back of the bottle, not the "fish oil" headline number.
5. **Caffeine** — 3–6 mg/kg pre-training. Coffee, espresso, pill, pre-workout — same molecule. Last dose at least 8 hours before bed.

### 7.2 Tier 2 — situational

- **Caffeine for hunger** — late-morning espresso when lunch is 2 hours away kills the urge to snack. 50–100 mg.
- **Psyllium husk fiber** — 5–10 g in water with meals when meal fiber is below 8 g. Helps satiety, glucose response, regularity.
- **Electrolyte mix** — sodium 1000 mg + potassium 400 mg + magnesium 100 mg in 500 ml water on conditioning days or whenever you feel flat. LMNT, Liquid IV (sugar-free), or DIY (salt + lite salt + 100 ml fruit juice).
- **Ashwagandha (KSM-66)** — 600 mg/day. Modest cortisol-blunting effect in stressed populations; may help with sleep. Drop if you notice nothing in 4 weeks.

### 7.3 Skip entirely (with one-line reasons)

- **Fat burners (anything with "thermo," "shred," "lipo")** — caffeine plus filler, often dosed dangerously, no advantage over coffee.
- **CLA (conjugated linoleic acid)** — meta-analyses show losses of 0.05 kg/week vs placebo at best, often null, with GI side effects.
- **Raspberry ketones** — animal model only, no human data at sane doses.
- **Garcinia cambogia** — null in human trials, hepatotoxic at high doses.
- **BCAAs (as a standalone)** — redundant if you hit total protein; whey already has them. (Wolfe 2017.)
- **Detox teas / "cleanses"** — laxatives. You will lose water and dignity, not fat.
- **Apple cider vinegar pills** — drink the vinegar in salad dressing; pills do nothing extra.
- **Carnitine** — small effects in carnitine-deficient populations only; null in healthy meat-eaters.

## 8. Troubleshooting (Fat-Loss Specific)

### 8.1 Scale stalled 2 weeks

The order is: **(a) audit, (b) cut food, (c) add steps, (d) reconsider deficit size**.

1. Re-weigh all food for 5 days. The average "stall" is a 200 kcal tracking creep — peanut butter spoons, dressing, oil splashes, restaurant guesses.
2. If audit is clean, cut 100–150 kcal from carbs only. Hold protein and fat.
3. If still flat after 1 more week, add 1500 steps/day before adding cardio. Walking is cheaper than recovery debt.
4. Only after (1)–(3) fail, consider that maintenance has dropped (metabolic adaptation, Trexler 2014) and cut another 100 kcal — but at this point, also pre-plan a 1-week diet break (see §8.7).
5. Do **not** add a fifth conditioning session or cut breakfast. Both wreck training and sleep.

### 8.2 Unmanageable hunger

Hunger ≠ failure. Hunger is the body asking for food. Manage it; don't moralize about it.

- **Volume swaps**: replace 50 g rice with 250 g cauliflower rice, replace beef with chicken breast at lunch, double the salad portion. Same kcal, twice the chew.
- **Fiber**: are you actually hitting 35–45 g? Add psyllium 5 g with the meal that hits hardest.
- **Water**: thirst masquerades as hunger. Drink 500 ml; wait 15 min.
- **Sleep**: under 7 hours and ghrelin spikes ~25%. Fix sleep before fixing diet.
- **Protein**: under 2.2 g/kg, hunger gets violent. Front-load protein at breakfast (40+ g).
- **Diet soda / coffee**: deploy mid-afternoon when willpower is lowest.
- **Chew gum** while cooking — most overeating happens during prep.
- **Stop weighing yourself daily** if it's driving you to undereat then binge. Move to weekly average.

### 8.3 Strength dropping

Some strength loss in a deficit is normal — 2–5% on top-end lifts. More than that is a flag.

- **Protein audit**: are you hitting 2.4 g/kg? Weigh it for 3 days.
- **Sleep audit**: under 7 hours, training output craters.
- **Refeed audit**: are you actually doing them at Week 5+? Many people skip out of fear of "ruining it."
- **Volume audit**: drop one set per exercise. Holding intensity (load) matters more than volume in a deficit.
- **If all four are clean**, your deficit is too aggressive — cut it by 100 kcal for two weeks.

### 8.4 Energy crash (3 pm / 5 pm wall)

- **Carbs at lunch**: are you eating a salad-only lunch and crashing later? Add 40 g rice or a piece of fruit.
- **Electrolytes**: sodium under 3 g/day is a guaranteed afternoon crash. Salt your eggs, salt your water.
- **Caffeine timing**: re-dose at 12:30 pm, not 3 pm (interferes with sleep).
- **Cold water on the face**, 5 min walk outside in sun. The biology of this is real.

### 8.5 Social events

The goal is to not derail a 12-week program for one dinner. Two real tools:

**Calorie banking**: cut 200 kcal/day for 3 days before the event — usually from fat (less oil, lean cuts only). Bank ~600 kcal. Save it for the dinner. Land back on plan the next morning. **Do not** bank more than 600 kcal — undereating before drinking is how people end up in the ER.

**Alcohol rules**:
- Alcohol is **7 kcal/g** — closer to fat than to carbs.
- Alcohol **acutely halts fat oxidation** for 8–12 hours while the liver clears it. Drink Friday, fat loss "pauses" until Saturday afternoon.
- Two-drink ceiling. Choose: wine (130 kcal/150 ml), spirits + soda (90 kcal/45 ml), light beer (100 kcal/355 ml). Skip cocktails (300+ kcal/glass), cream liqueurs, and beer after the second.
- Drink water 1:1 between drinks. Eat protein-anchored food while drinking (not after).
- **Never drink the day before a heavy lift**. Sleep architecture wrecks for 36 hours.

**Restaurant tactics at events**: see §6.

### 8.6 Plateau on weight but inches dropping

This is **good**. Body recomposition is real, especially in months 2–3 of training for intermediate lifters in a moderate deficit. Trust the tape measure and clothing fit over the scale for 2 weeks. If photos and tape both stall for 2 weeks, return to §8.1.

### 8.7 Optional diet break

At **Week 6** or **Week 10** (your choice — pick the harder one psychologically), take a **7-day diet break at maintenance** (~+450 kcal/day, all from carbs and a little fat). This is not a cheat week. Track food at maintenance.

- Restores thyroid output (T3) and leptin.
- Improves training output for the rest of the block.
- Improves long-term adherence — the people who do diet breaks finish the program.
- Expect 0.5–1.5 kg scale bump from glycogen + water. It is not fat. It will come off in 7 days back in deficit.

Reference: Byrne et al. 2018 (MATADOR study) showed intermittent (2-on, 2-off) dieting preserved more lean mass and produced greater fat loss than continuous dieting over the same total deficit days. We use a single mid-program break because adherence is the real fight.
