# Hypertrophy 12-Week Mesocycle — 14-Day Companion Diet

Coach- and chef-built nutrition program engineered for the TJFit Hypertrophy 12-Week Mesocycle (5 sessions/wk, 70 min, Push/Pull/Legs/Upper/Lower). This is not a generic meal plan. Numbers are calibrated for an intermediate male lifter, ~75 kg reference body, training six days a week with one full rest day. Scale rules at the top. Cook the food. Hit the macros. Get bigger.

---

## 1. Overview & Macros

### 1.1 Estimating maintenance calories

Use Mifflin–St Jeor for resting metabolic rate (RMR), then apply an activity multiplier.

**Mifflin–St Jeor (men):** RMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) + 5
**Mifflin–St Jeor (women):** RMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) − 161

Activity multiplier for this program (5 lifting sessions + general daily walking, desk job): **1.55**.

**Worked example — 80 kg, 180 cm, 28-year-old male:**
- RMR = (10 × 80) + (6.25 × 180) − (5 × 28) + 5 = 800 + 1125 − 140 + 5 = **1790 kcal**
- Maintenance = 1790 × 1.55 = **2775 kcal**
- Hypertrophy surplus = +300 kcal → **Target = 3075 kcal/day**

Surplus prescription: **+200 kcal** if you carry visible bodyfat already, **+300 kcal** if you are lean and gaining slowly, **+400 kcal** only if you are a hardgainer with a measured 2-week weight stall. Hard cap: 450 g/week scale gain. Anything faster is fat.

### 1.2 Macro split — training days (5/wk)

40% carbs / 30% protein / 30% fat. Protein anchored at **2.0 g/kg**.

| Tier | BW | kcal | Protein (g) | Carbs (g) | Fat (g) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 65 kg | 2600 | 145 | 260 | 87 |
| Reference | 80 kg | 3075 | 175 | 308 | 103 |
| Heavy | 95 kg | 3550 | 205 | 355 | 118 |

### 1.3 Macro split — rest days (1/wk)

Drop calories by ~250 kcal: carbs come down, fat stays moderate, protein unchanged. 30% carbs / 35% protein / 35% fat.

| Tier | BW | kcal | Protein (g) | Carbs (g) | Fat (g) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 65 kg | 2350 | 145 | 180 | 92 |
| Reference | 80 kg | 2825 | 175 | 215 | 110 |
| Heavy | 95 kg | 3300 | 205 | 250 | 128 |

### 1.4 Meal timing

- **4 fixed meals + 1 optional post-workout shake** on training days.
- Breakfast within 60 min of waking.
- Pre-workout snack 60–90 min before lifting: 40–60 g carbs + 25–30 g protein, low fat.
- Post-workout shake within 45 min: 35 g whey + 50 g carbs (banana + 30 g oats or 200 ml juice).
- Last meal 90 min before bed. Include 30–40 g slow protein (cottage cheese, casein, or Greek yogurt).
- Keep meals ~3 hours apart. Do not skip breakfast on training days.

### 1.5 Hydration, electrolytes, fiber, micronutrients

- **Water:** 35 ml/kg/day floor. 80 kg → 2.8 L. Add 500 ml per hour of training.
- **Sodium:** 3–5 g/day. Salt your food. Add 1 g (~½ tsp) into your pre-workout drink.
- **Potassium:** 3.5 g/day from bananas, potatoes, leafy greens, yogurt.
- **Magnesium:** 400 mg/day. Pumpkin seeds, almonds, dark chocolate, supplement if needed.
- **Fiber:** 14 g per 1000 kcal. ~40 g/day at 3000 kcal.
- **Micronutrient floor:** 2 fruits + 400 g vegetables daily, non-negotiable. One serving of leafy greens, one serving of cruciferous, one colored vegetable.

## 2. The 14-Day Meal Plan

All macros target the **80 kg reference tier (3075 kcal training / 2825 kcal rest)**. Scale linearly by bodyweight ratio (e.g. 95 kg = multiply gram weights by 1.19). Days 1–6, 8–13 = training. Day 7 and 14 = rest (lower carbs, higher fat ratio).

Meal slot key: **B** = Breakfast, **L** = Lunch, **P** = Pre-workout, **D** = Dinner, **S** = Optional post-workout shake.

### Day 1 (Push) — Mediterranean

**1B — Greek Yogurt Power Bowl** | 620 kcal · P 45 · C 75 · F 15
- 350 g 2% Greek yogurt, 80 g rolled oats (dry), 1 banana (120 g), 20 g walnuts, 20 g honey, 5 g cinnamon, 80 g blueberries.
- Cook time: 5 min. Easy.
1. Toast oats dry in a pan over medium heat 3 min until fragrant.
2. Layer yogurt in a bowl.
3. Slice banana over top.
4. Crush walnuts, scatter.
5. Drizzle honey, dust with cinnamon, finish with blueberries.
- Seasoning: Ceylon cinnamon, pinch of flaky sea salt.
- Alt: No walnuts → 25 g almonds. No blueberries → 100 g strawberries.
- Coach note: Casein-dominant yogurt + slow oats blunts morning cortisol. Stable energy until lunch.

**1L — Chicken Souvlaki Bowl** | 780 kcal · P 55 · C 90 · F 20
- 180 g chicken thigh (skinless, boneless), 200 g cooked basmati rice (70 g dry), 150 g cucumber, 100 g cherry tomatoes, 80 g red onion, 100 g tzatziki, 10 ml olive oil, 1 lemon.
- Cook time: 20 min. Easy.
1. Cube chicken, toss with oil, 1 tsp oregano, 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp salt, juice of ½ lemon.
2. Heat skillet on high. Sear chicken 4 min per side until 75 °C / 165 °F internal.
3. Cook rice in 140 ml water + pinch of salt, 18 min covered.
4. Dice cucumber and tomato. Slice onion thin.
5. Plate rice, chicken, vegetables. Spoon tzatziki. Finish with lemon juice.
- Seasoning: Greek rub (oregano, paprika, garlic, lemon, salt).
- Alt: Chicken thigh → 200 g chicken breast. Basmati → 230 g cooked quinoa.
- Coach note: Thigh meat has 30% more zinc and iron than breast. Better recovery for high-volume push days.

**1P — Rice Cakes + Whey** | 380 kcal · P 35 · C 50 · F 4
- 4 plain rice cakes (40 g), 25 g honey, 30 g whey isolate, 200 ml water.
- 3 min. Easy.
1. Drizzle honey on rice cakes.
2. Shake whey with cold water in a shaker.
3. Drink shake, eat rice cakes 75 min before lifting.
- Alt: Rice cakes → 80 g sourdough toast. Whey → 200 g 0% Greek yogurt.
- Coach note: Fast carbs + fast protein. Low fat for gastric emptying.

**1D — Lamb Kofta with Tabbouleh** | 850 kcal · P 50 · C 70 · F 35
- 180 g lean ground lamb (10% fat), 60 g bulgur (dry), 1 bunch parsley (40 g), 1 tomato (120 g), ½ red onion (60 g), 15 ml olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 30 g tahini, 1 pita (60 g).
- 25 min. Medium.
1. Mix lamb with ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp coriander, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp black pepper, grated ¼ onion.
2. Form into 6 logs around skewers.
3. Soak bulgur in 120 ml boiling water 12 min, drain.
4. Finely chop parsley, tomato, remaining onion. Fluff into bulgur with lemon and oil.
5. Grill kofta on high heat 3 min per side.
6. Mix tahini with 30 ml water, 1 clove garlic crushed, lemon juice, salt.
7. Serve kofta over tabbouleh with pita and tahini sauce.
- Seasoning: Shawarma-adjacent (cumin, coriander, cinnamon, black pepper).
- Alt: Lamb → 180 g 90% lean beef. Bulgur → 70 g dry couscous.
- Coach note: Heme iron + creatine from lamb beats chicken on Push days.

### Day 2 (Pull) — Asian

**2B — Japanese Tamago Rice Bowl** | 600 kcal · P 40 · C 75 · F 14
- 3 large eggs, 200 g cooked short-grain rice, 30 g spinach, 10 ml sesame oil, 10 ml soy sauce, 5 g furikake, 1 spring onion.
- 10 min. Easy.
1. Whisk eggs with 10 ml soy, pinch of sugar.
2. Heat half the sesame oil in a non-stick pan on medium-low.
3. Pour eggs in thirds, rolling each layer (tamago technique). Total 5 min.
4. Wilt spinach in remaining oil 60 sec.
5. Plate rice, slice tamago, top with spinach, furikake, spring onion.
- Alt: Furikake → toasted sesame + ½ sheet crumbled nori.
- Coach note: Egg yolks load choline; supports grip-intensive pulling.

**2L — Korean Beef Bibimbap** | 800 kcal · P 55 · C 85 · F 22
- 170 g lean beef sirloin, 200 g cooked rice, 80 g carrots, 80 g spinach, 60 g bean sprouts, 1 egg, 15 ml gochujang, 10 ml sesame oil, 10 ml soy, 1 clove garlic, 5 g sesame seeds.
- 25 min. Medium.
1. Slice beef thin, marinate 10 min in soy, half the sesame oil, garlic, ½ tsp brown sugar.
2. Julienne carrots, sauté 3 min with salt.
3. Wilt spinach 60 sec in hot pan, squeeze dry.
4. Blanch sprouts 60 sec in salted boiling water.
5. Sear beef 90 sec per side in hot pan.
6. Fry egg sunny-side up.
7. Arrange rice, vegetables in zones, beef center, egg on top.
8. Mix gochujang with 5 ml sesame oil; spoon over.
- Seasoning: Korean rub (gochujang, soy, sesame, garlic).
- Alt: Sirloin → 180 g flank steak. Gochujang → 10 g sriracha + 5 g miso.
- Coach note: Iron + B12 from sirloin supports erythropoiesis after high-volume pulling.

**2P — Banana Oat Smoothie** | 400 kcal · P 35 · C 55 · F 5
- 1 banana (120 g), 40 g oats, 30 g whey, 250 ml skim milk, ice.
- 3 min. Easy.
1. Blend everything 30 sec.
2. Drink 75 min pre-workout.
- Alt: Skim milk → 250 ml almond milk + 20 g extra whey.
- Coach note: Cheap, repeatable, hits the macros without chewing.

**2D — Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapow)** | 820 kcal · P 55 · C 85 · F 25
- 180 g chicken thigh, 200 g cooked jasmine rice, 80 g green beans, 1 egg, 10 g garlic, 15 g Thai chili (or 5 g if heat-shy), 30 g Thai basil, 15 ml fish sauce, 10 ml oyster sauce, 5 g sugar, 15 ml peanut oil.
- 15 min. Medium.
1. Mince chicken or chop very fine. Smash garlic and chili in a mortar.
2. Heat peanut oil in a wok until smoking.
3. Throw in garlic-chili paste 15 sec.
4. Add chicken, stir-fry 4 min until browned.
5. Add green beans, fish sauce, oyster sauce, sugar. 2 min.
6. Off heat, fold in basil.
7. Fry egg in second pan, crispy edges.
8. Plate rice, chicken, top with egg.
- Seasoning: Thai trinity — fish sauce, oyster sauce, palm sugar.
- Alt: Thai basil → Italian basil + ½ tsp anise seed. Chicken thigh → 180 g shrimp.
- Coach note: Fish sauce delivers glutamic acid; deepens umami, helps appetite when chasing surplus.

### Day 3 (Legs) — American

3. Soft scramble eggs in butter on low heat 4 min.
4. Toast sourdough.
5. Halve tomatoes, dust with salt.
6. Slice steak against grain, plate everything.
- Seasoning: Black pepper, flaky sea salt, smoked paprika dust on the eggs.
- Alt: Flank → 150 g sirloin tip. Sourdough → 80 g rye.
- Coach note: Leg day demands ATP and creatine — front-load both before noon.

**3L — Loaded Sweet Potato + Turkey Chili** | 820 kcal · P 55 · C 95 · F 22

2. Brown turkey 5 min.
3. Add taco blend, beans, corn, tomato. Simmer 12 min.
4. Split potato, fluff flesh.
5. Top with chili, cheese, sour cream, cilantro.
- Alt: Turkey → 170 g 93% lean beef. Sweet potato → 250 g baked white potato.
- Coach note: Sweet potato glycemic curve is gentle — won't crash you before evening squats if eaten 4 h prior.

**3P — PB&J on Bagel** | 420 kcal · P 25 · C 60 · F 10

- 4 min. Easy.

2. Spread peanut butter on one half, jam on the other.
3. Drink whey on the side.

**3D — Smash Burgers + Smashed Potatoes** | 900 kcal · P 55 · C 75 · F 38
- 170 g 80/20 ground beef (2 patties), 1 brioche bun (70 g), 30 g cheddar, 60 g iceberg, 2 slices tomato, 20 g pickles, 15 g mustard, 250 g baby potatoes, 15 ml olive oil, 5 g rosemary.
- 30 min. Medium.
1. Parboil potatoes 12 min in salted water. Drain, smash with the back of a glass.
2. Toss with oil, salt, rosemary. Roast at 220 °C / 425 °F for 18 min.
3. Form beef into 2 loose balls. Don't season yet.
4. Cast iron screaming hot. Smash each ball flat with a spatula for 90 sec.
5. Salt, flip 60 sec, cheese on, lid 30 sec.
6. Toast bun in beef fat.
7. Build: bun, mustard, pickles, patties, iceberg, tomato, bun.
- Seasoning: Maldon salt, fresh black pepper. That's it — let the beef talk.
- Alt: 80/20 beef → 170 g 85/15 + 10 g butter. Brioche → whole wheat bun.
- Coach note: Highest-fat meal of the day after heaviest workout. Calorie payload + dopamine reward = adherence.

### Day 5 (Lower) — Indian

**5B — Masala Egg Bhurji on Toast** | 640 kcal · P 42 · C 55 · F 28

**5D — Dal Tadka + Jeera Rice + Tandoori Salmon** | 850 kcal · P 55 · C 90 · F 26

## 5. Cooking Primer

### 5.1 Sunday batch-cook (90 minutes total)

1. **Rice (15 min hands-on, 20 cooking):** 600 g basmati, 600 g jasmine. Store in airtight containers. Fridge 4 days, freezer 3 months.
2. **Roast 1.5 kg chicken thighs (5 min prep, 35 oven):** salt + olive oil + garlic at 200 °C / 400 °F. Pull at 75 °C / 165 °F internal.
3. **Roast 1 sweet potato + 500 g baby potatoes:** same oven, 40 min.
4. **Hard-boil 6 eggs:** rolling boil 8 min, ice bath, peel under water.
5. **Make 1 sauce + 1 spice blend** from below.
6. **Pre-portion lunches into 5 containers.**

### 5.2 Storage rules

| Item | Fridge | Freezer |
|---|---|---|
| Cooked rice | 4 days | 3 months |
| Cooked chicken | 4 days | 3 months |
| Cooked beef | 3 days | 3 months |
| Cooked fish | 2 days | 2 months |
| Hard-boiled egg (peeled) | 5 days | no |
| Pre-cut vegetables | 3 days | no |
| Dressings/sauces | 5–7 days | no (oil emulsions break) |
| Yogurt-based marinades | 2 days | no |
| Cooked beans/lentils | 5 days | 3 months |

### 5.3 Spice blends (make once, use everywhere)

Mix in 100 g batches, store in airtight jar 6 months.

1. **Shawarma rub:** 30 g cumin, 20 g coriander, 15 g paprika, 10 g turmeric, 10 g garlic powder, 5 g cinnamon, 5 g black pepper, 5 g cardamom.
2. **Taco / fajita blend:** 30 g chili powder, 20 g cumin, 15 g paprika, 10 g garlic powder, 10 g onion powder, 5 g oregano, 5 g black pepper, 5 g salt.
3. **Garam masala:** 25 g cumin, 25 g coriander, 15 g cardamom, 10 g cinnamon, 10 g black pepper, 5 g cloves, 5 g nutmeg, 5 g bay (ground).
4. **Italian herb mix:** 25 g oregano, 25 g basil, 20 g thyme, 15 g rosemary, 10 g marjoram, 5 g garlic powder.
5. **Greek rub:** 30 g oregano, 20 g garlic powder, 15 g onion powder, 15 g paprika, 10 g black pepper, 10 g salt.
6. **Cajun blend:** 25 g paprika, 20 g garlic powder, 15 g onion powder, 10 g cayenne, 10 g oregano, 10 g thyme, 10 g black pepper.
7. **Steak rub:** 40 g kosher salt, 30 g coarse black pepper, 15 g garlic powder, 10 g rosemary, 5 g brown sugar.
8. **Korean BBQ rub (dry):** 25 g brown sugar, 20 g garlic powder, 15 g ginger powder, 15 g gochugaru, 15 g sesame, 10 g salt.

### 5.4 Five universal sauces

1. **Tahini sauce (200 g):** 100 g tahini, 60 ml water, 30 ml lemon, 1 garlic clove crushed, ¼ tsp salt, ¼ tsp cumin. Use on bowls, kofta, falafel.

3. **Chimichurri (200 g):** 1 bunch parsley fine-chopped, 4 cloves garlic, 100 ml olive oil, 30 ml red wine vinegar, 5 g oregano, 5 g chili flakes, ½ tsp salt. Use on steak, eggs, potatoes.

5. **Salsa verde (Mexican, 300 g):** 6 tomatillos charred, 1 jalapeño, 2 cloves garlic, ½ onion, 1 bunch cilantro, juice of 1 lime, salt. Blend. Use on chicken, fish, eggs.

## 7. Supplements

### Tier 1 — proven, daily

- **Creatine monohydrate, 5 g/day.** Any time. Loading not needed. Increases muscle creatine 20–40%, output and lean mass measurable at 4 weeks (Kreider 2017, ISSN position stand).
- **Whey protein, 25–40 g per serving as needed.** Convenience, not magic. Use to hit daily protein floor.
- **Vitamin D3, 2000–4000 IU/day** (more in winter or if indoors). Test 25(OH)D, aim 30–50 ng/ml.
- **Omega-3 (EPA+DHA), 2–3 g/day total.** Supports muscle protein synthesis (Smith 2011) and inflammation.

### Tier 2 — situational

- **Caffeine, 3–6 mg/kg** 45–60 min pre-workout. Skip if training after 18:00 or you're a slow metabolizer.
- **Magnesium glycinate, 300–400 mg** evening. Sleep + cramps.
- **Electrolyte powder** during sessions >60 min or hot environments. ~500 mg sodium + 200 mg potassium per liter.

### Skip entirely

- **BCAAs / EAAs powder.** Redundant if you hit 1.8+ g/kg whole-food protein.
- **Fat burners.** Cost > benefit. Caffeine + diet does the same job.
- **Testosterone boosters (tribulus, ZMA hype, ashwagandha at hypertrophy doses).** Effects are too small or unproven to bank on. Sleep and protein move the needle further.
- **Glutamine.** No measurable benefit in non-clinical lifters.

## 8. Troubleshooting

### Not gaining weight after 2 weeks
1. Re-weigh food. Most underreporting is in oils and nut butters.
2. Add 250 ml whole milk + 30 g oats to a daily shake (~250 kcal).
3. Move pre-workout snack to a full second lunch.
4. Audit sleep — under 7 h kills appetite and recovery.

### Gaining too fast (>500 g/week sustained for 3 weeks)
1. Drop pre-workout snack by 100 kcal.
2. Trim one dinner carb portion by 50 g rice.
3. Hold for 2 weeks. Re-evaluate.

### GI distress from high volume
1. Split meals smaller and more frequent (5–6 instead of 4).
2. Reduce raw cruciferous vegetables, cook them instead.
3. Soak legumes overnight, rinse twice.
4. Take meals with 250 ml water, not a liter.

### Low appetite
- Use calorie-dense additions: nut butters (20 g = 120 kcal), olive oil (15 ml = 130 kcal), dried fruit (40 g = 130 kcal), full-fat dairy.
- Liquid calories: 500 kcal smoothie (200 ml milk + 30 g oats + 30 g whey + 1 banana + 30 g peanut butter).
- Stop training fasted — kills daily intake.

### Travel
- Default to the airport fallback meal.
- Long flights: pre-pack 2 chicken-rice containers (TSA-allowed when sealed and frozen). Pack whey.
- Hotel room: rice cooker + microwaveable rice pouches + canned tuna + greens from the grocery is a 700-kcal meal in 8 min.

### Illness
- Cut volume by 30%. Protein unchanged. Carbs to support recovery. Drop training, not eating.

### Social events
- Eat 80% of your target before going. Drink water-spaced alcohol (1:1).
- Beer is 150 kcal/can — count it. Wine 120 kcal/glass.
- One day off-plan in 14 will not derail you. Two will.
