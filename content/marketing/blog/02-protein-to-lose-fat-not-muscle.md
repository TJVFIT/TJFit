# EN — How Much Protein Do You Actually Need to Lose Fat Without Losing Muscle?

Everyone agrees protein matters in a cut. Almost nobody gives you a number. Here's the number — and why it changes with your goal.

## The short answer

To lose fat while holding onto muscle, aim for roughly **1.0 to 1.3 grams of protein per pound of bodyweight per day**. The harder and leaner the cut, the higher in that range you go.

| Goal | Protein target |
|---|---|
| Aggressive / contest-style cut | 1.2–1.3 g/lb |
| Standard fat loss | 1.0 g/lb |
| Recomp (lose fat, build muscle) | 1.1 g/lb |
| Lean bulk | 1.0 g/lb |
| Beginner / maintenance | 0.8–0.9 g/lb |

These aren't made up — they're the exact targets TJFit's programs prescribe, from the Fat Loss Protocol up to the advanced Peak Cut.

## Why protein goes *up* when calories go *down*

It sounds backwards: eat less overall, but eat *more* protein. Here's the logic.

When you're in a calorie deficit, your body is looking for fuel — and muscle is on the menu unless you defend it. Two things defend it: **lifting** (the stimulus to keep muscle) and **protein** (the raw material). High protein does three jobs at once in a cut:

1. **Preserves muscle** so the weight you lose is fat, not the tissue that gives you shape.
2. **Keeps you full** — protein is the most satiating macro, which makes the deficit livable.
3. **Costs energy to digest** — its thermic effect is higher than carbs or fat.

Cut protein to save calories and you'll lose weight faster on the scale — and a chunk of it will be muscle. That's how people end up "skinny fat" at the end of a diet.

## How to actually hit it

- Anchor every meal around a protein source: eggs, chicken, lean beef, fish, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey.
- A shake covers the gap on busy days — one scoop is ~25–30g.
- Spread it across 4 meals rather than one giant dinner; your body uses it better.

You don't need to calculate this alone. Every TJFit bundle sets your protein target by goal and builds the meals around it.

# TR — Kas Kaybetmeden Yağ Yakmak İçin Gerçekte Ne Kadar Protein Gerekli?

Kası korurken yağ yakmak için günde **kilo başına yaklaşık 1,0–1,3 gram protein** hedefle. Diyet ne kadar sert ve yağsızsa, bu aralıkta o kadar yukarı çıkarsın.

| Recomp (yağ yak, kas yap) | 1,1 g/lb |

## Kalori *düşerken* protein neden *artıyor?*

Ters gibi geliyor: toplamda daha az ye ama *daha fazla* protein al. Mantığı şu.

Kalori açığındayken vücudun yakıt arar — ve onu savunmazsan kas menüdedir. İki şey savunur: **ağırlık çalışmak** (kası tutma uyarısı) ve **protein** (ham madde). Diyette yüksek protein aynı anda üç iş yapar:

1. **Kası korur** — böylece kaybettiğin kilo yağ olur, sana şekil veren doku değil.
2. **Seni tok tutar** — protein en doyurucu makro, bu da açığı yaşanabilir kılar.
3. **Sindirimi enerji ister** — termik etkisi karbonhidrat ve yağdan yüksektir.

Kalori kısmak için proteini kesersen tartıda daha hızlı kilo verirsin — ve bunun bir kısmı kas olur. İnsanların diyet sonunda "zayıf ama yağlı" kalmasının sebebi budur.
