# EN — Gym vs. Home for Fat Loss: Which 12-Week Program Actually Fits You?

"Should I join a gym or just train at home?" is the wrong first question. The right one is: **which structure will you actually finish?** Fat loss is a 12-week problem, not a one-workout problem, and the plan you stick to beats the plan that's theoretically optimal. Here's an honest comparison of TJFit's two fat-loss paths.

## The gym path: Fat Loss Protocol (free)

Five sessions a week, resistance-led, built in three phases — **Prime** (strength base, modest deficit), **Strip** (higher-density work, sharper deficit, a cardio block), and **Polish** (refeed weeks and a definition finish). Protein sits at 1g per pound of bodyweight and the deficit runs about 15% under maintenance, with two refeed weeks built in so your metabolism and your motivation don't crater at week 6.

**Best for you if:** you have gym access, you can commit five days, and you want the fastest muscle-preserving cut. Barbells and machines let you load progressively, which is what protects muscle while the fat comes off.

## The home path: Home Fat Loss Starter

Four short bodyweight sessions a week — squats, push-ups, inverted rows off a sturdy table, reverse lunges — across **Habit → Build → Push** phases. Nutrition uses plate-based portions with no food scale for the first four weeks, on a gentler 10% deficit. It's the on-ramp: designed so month 3 looks nothing like month 1 even without a single dumbbell.

**Best for you if:** you have no gym, an unpredictable schedule, or you're returning after a long break and want to rebuild the habit before the intensity.

## The honest verdict

- **Fastest result, have equipment, 5 days free →** Fat Loss Protocol.
- **No equipment, need flexibility, rebuilding the habit →** Home Fat Loss Starter.

Neither wins on paper — the one you finish wins. The gym Fat Loss Protocol is **free**, so if you have access there's no reason not to start there.
